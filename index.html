How this is built

Six pages of hand-written HTML, one stylesheet, one small script per page — no framework and no build step. Every tool keeps its own state in this browser: the ledger and the study progress in localStorage, the music list only while the tab is open, the tarot draw just to fill the strip above. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
